About FoodBridge

A Hyperlocal, Event-Aware Surplus Food Rescue and Demand-Aggregation Platform with Multi-Stage Chain-of-Custody Verification.

The Core Problem

While food rescue platforms like Olio, Too Good To Go, Food Rescue US, and Food Rescue Hero operate globally, existing systems either rely on static directory lookups, fixed 1:1 recurring corporate routes, or manual WhatsApp coordination. In institutional environments like university campuses, food surplus is bursty, highly perishable, and fragmented across multiple small kitchens (hostel messes, symposiums, canteens).

FoodBridge innovates through an autonomous coordination engine that combines multi-donor aggregation, dynamic urgency decay timers, and verified cryptographic handshakes.

The 5 Signature Technical Pillars

1. Multi-Factor Need-to-Surplus Matching

Instead of simple proximity, candidates are scored using a weighted multi-factor heuristic: Score = 0.35(Distance) + 0.30(Urgency) + 0.20(Capacity) + 0.15(Dietary).

2. Multi-Donor Resource Aggregation (N:1 Knapsack Pooling)

When an NGO shelter requires 200 meals, the algorithm pools surplus from multiple small donors (e.g. 70 + 80 + 50 meals) into a unified multi-stop volunteer manifest.

3. Dynamic Food Urgency & Spoilage Decay

Classifies listings into 4 real-time urgency tiers (Green > 4h, Yellow 2-4h, Orange 1-2h, Red < 1h), dynamically expanding volunteer notification radii as spoilage approaches.

4. Campus Event Fast-Track Mode

1-Click rapid surplus declaration interface tailored specifically for college symposiums, cultural fests, hackathons, and hostel messes.

5. Dual-Stage Cryptographic Chain-of-Custody (CoC)

Enforces physical QR and 6-digit OTP verification at both Donor pickup and NGO handover points to guarantee safety and prevent fraud.
