Our Mission

Bridging Food Waste & Hunger

FoodBridge connects institutional surplus food from college messes, events, and caterers with nearby verified shelters in real time.

Time-Sensitive Rescue

Cooked food has a narrow window for safe consumption. FoodBridge prioritizes pickups by urgency so surplus never goes to waste.

Pooled Donations

Shelters needing 200 meals can easily receive matching portions from 2-3 nearby donors instead of waiting for a single large sponsor.

Verified Handover

Every handover is verified with simple verification codes between donors, couriers, and shelters to ensure food arrives safely.

Measured Impact

Track exact kilograms of food saved, meals delivered, and carbon footprint prevented from landfills on our transparent dashboard.
