Architectural Novelty

Why FoodBridge is a Superior Coordination Engine

Engineered to overcome the limitations of static food directories and manual WhatsApp chats.

1. Multi-Donor N:1 Pooling

An automated knapsack algorithm aggregates surplus from 3-4 small donors (e.g. 70 + 80 + 50 meals) to satisfy a large 200-meal requirement for a single shelter.

2. Dynamic 4-Tier Urgency Decay

Calculates real-time perishability timers (Green $\rightarrow$ Yellow $\rightarrow$ Orange $\rightarrow$ Red) and automatically expands volunteer dispatch radii as spoilage approaches.

3. Campus Event Fast-Track

1-Click declaration workflow specifically optimized for university symposiums, fests, hackathons, and hostel messes with student volunteer routing.

4. Dual-Stage Cryptographic Chain-of-Custody

Enforces physical QR and 6-digit OTP verification at both Donor pickup and NGO handover points to prevent fraud, ensure food safety, and maintain an immutable audit trail.

5. Real-Time Need Heatmap & Capacity

Visualizes spatial demand intensity across urban zones while allowing shelters to publish live storage telemetry and prevent food overfilling.
