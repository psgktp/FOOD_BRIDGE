How It Works

A streamlined 3-step process from kitchen surplus to table.

1. Post Surplus

Donors enter remaining meal count, address, and safe consumption time window.

2. Smart Match

Nearby verified shelters receive instant notifications and claim matching quantities.

3. Verified Handover

Volunteers pick up and deliver the packages using simple verification codes.
